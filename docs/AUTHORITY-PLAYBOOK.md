# Authority & GEO-Citation Playbook — Dental Med Austria

Backlinks/authority is the one ranking factor no on-site tool can generate. This is the
researched, **verified** plan (every URL was checked via live web research, 2026-07-01).
Work it top-to-bottom; the phases are ordered by impact-per-hour.

**The strategic picture the research uncovered:**
- DMA is **already listed on Bookimed** ("Dental Med Austria & Elixence") — but the profile is thin.
- DMA is **missing entirely** from WhatClinic, Dental Departures' Albania Top-10, and MedicalTourismCo's shortlists — pages that rank top-5 for "best dental clinic albania" and that AI engines quote.
- Competitor **Zahnklinik Tirana** built its German authority with paid advertorials on Handelsblatt, WirtschaftsWoche and forbes.swiss — a repeatable, buyable playbook.
- **Dentaly.org has NO Albania page** and Die Zahnarztempfehlung's Albania category is **EMPTY** — two first-mover slots.
- Albania is underrepresented on Reddit/forums vs Turkey — today's honest threads become next year's AI citations.

## Hard rules (protect the domain)

- **No fake reviews, no astroturfing, no undisclosed accounts** — Trustpilot/Tripadvisor/Reddit detect and ban it; a single scandal kills the trust wedge that IS the DMA brand.
- Reviews are **asked for, never bought or gated** (EU law + platform rules).
- One consistent NAP (name/address/phone) across every listing = `Dental Med Austria, Rruga Kristo Luarasi, Tirana` + one phone number.
- Every profile links to `https://www.dentalmedaustria.al` (www, https) and where possible to the matching language section (`/de`, `/it`).
- Track everything in a simple sheet: platform / date / login owner / profile URL / review count / last refreshed.
