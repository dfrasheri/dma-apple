## TL;DR (the one-paragraph version)

Search is splitting into two surfaces — the classic Google results page and a new layer of **generative engines** (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini). They are won by *the same underlying assets*: a strong **entity** (who you are), demonstrable **E-E-A-T** (medical trust), **structured data** (machine-readable facts), and a clean **content architecture**. On top of that shared foundation, you run two playbooks. The proven SEO playbook earns rankings and the local pack. The GEO playbook earns *citations* inside AI answers — and the single biggest lever there is **publishing original, proprietary statistics** (you already have killers: 38,000 implants, 98% success, 8,000 patients). Do both, and you capture demand at the exact moment ~70% of medical-tourism patients begin: an online search.

## Part 1 — Why this matters now (the shift)

- **Zero-click is the norm.** ~**58.5%** of U.S. Google searches end without a click (59.7% in Europe). Inside AI environments it's far higher — **60–93%** zero-click. The answer *is* the destination now. ([digitalapplied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/zero-click-search-statistics-2026-complete-data), [Superlines](https://www.superlines.io/articles/ai-search-statistics/))
- **AI search is real and converts.** ChatGPT has ~**810M daily users**; Google AI Overviews reach **1.5B monthly**. AI referral traffic is still only ~**1% of total** but growing ~1%/month — and it **converts at 14.2% vs 2.8%** for ordinary organic. AI visitors arrive pre-qualified. ([Exposure Ninja](https://exposureninja.com/blog/ai-search-statistics/), [Superlines](https://www.superlines.io/articles/ai-search-statistics/))
- **Medical tourism is search-led.** ~**70% of international patients start their journey with an online search.** Being the cited/ranked answer is the whole game. ([Medical Tourism Magazine](https://www.magazine.medicaltourism.com/article/utilizing-search-engine-optimization-to-increase-visibility-for-medical-tourists))

**Implication for DMA:** you are not choosing between "SEO" and "AI." You are building one asset base that wins both, aimed at a high-intent, cross-border audience.

## Part 2 — How the machines actually pick winners

Generative engines do **not** share a brain. Optimizing for one is not optimizing for all:

- **Only ~11%** of cited domains overlap between ChatGPT and Perplexity; even Google's own AI Overviews vs AI Mode share URLs just **13.7%** of the time. ([authoritytech](https://authoritytech.io/curated/ai-citation-11-percent-platform-overlap-per-engine-audit-2026), [tryprofound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns))
- **ChatGPT** leans **encyclopedic** — Wikipedia is ~**47.9%** of top citations; brands with active **Trustpilot/G2/Capterra** profiles see **3× higher** citation rates.
- **Perplexity** leans **community** — **Reddit ~46.7%**, and it cites **2.76× more sources** per answer than ChatGPT.
- **Google AI Overviews** lean **video** — **YouTube** presence (brand in titles/transcripts) is the *single strongest* correlating signal studied. ([tryprofound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns), [Leapd](https://www.leapd.ai/blog/ai-visibility/how-chatgpt-google-ai-overviews-and-perplexity-source-information-in-2026))

**The one universal predictor:** across *every* engine, the strongest correlate of being cited is whether your page contains **original, proprietary data or statistics.** That is the headline finding to build the whole GEO strategy around.

## Part 3 — The proven foundation (SEO that already works)

### 3.1 Entity + E-E-A-T (non-negotiable for medical/YMYL)
Dentistry is textbook **YMYL** ("Your Money or Your Life") — Google applies its strictest quality bar. Proven requirements: content authored/reviewed by a **licensed practitioner**, visible **author bios + credentials**, citations, and **"last reviewed" dates.** In 2025 Google explicitly elevated **Experience** (firsthand, real-world) alongside formal expertise. ([Practice Builders](https://www.practicebuilders.com/blog/what-is-ymyl-and-what-its-impact-on-healthcare-seo/), [Rise](https://rise.co/blog/ranking-for-trust-how-googles-e-e-a-t-updates-are-changing-healthcare-seo))
- **For DMA:** make **Dr. Mentor Zeqja a named, credentialed author/reviewer** on treatment pages ("Medically reviewed by Dr. Mentor Zeqja, Orthodontist, 30+ yrs, Weiss University Vienna"). This is one of the highest-ROI moves you can make.

### 3.2 Structured data (the proven CTR + GEO substrate)
Rich results earn **~58% of clicks** vs 41% for plain results; pages that render as rich results see up to **+82% CTR** (Nestlé case), and FAQ rich results show very high engagement. Typical lift: **10–35%.** ([Sixth City](https://www.sixthcitymarketing.com/2023/12/20/schema-markup-statistics-facts/), [webselect](https://webselect.agency/schema-markup-s-effect-on-click-through-rate/))
- **Already shipped on the clone:** `Dentist`/LocalBusiness, `MedicalProcedure`, `FAQPage`, `ItemList`, `BreadcrumbList`, sitemap.xml, robots.txt.

### 3.3 Local SEO (proven weighting)
Local Pack ranking weight (Whitespark/BrightLocal 2025): **Google Business Profile 32%**, on-page 19%, **reviews 16%**, links 15%, behavioral 8%, citations 7%. Top individual GBP factors: **primary category, proximity, and keywords in the business title.** Credibility threshold: **15–20 reviews** minimum; **50+ reviews at 4.3★+** to compete for top-3. Verified profiles **with fresh photos/video** get measurably more calls and visits. ([Search Atlas](https://searchatlas.com/blog/local-ranking-factors/), [BiziQ](https://biziq.com/blog/local-seo-statistics/))

### 3.4 Topical authority via content clusters
Patient-first content organized into **clusters** (a pillar + supporting pages) builds topical authority — exactly the structure of the **catalogue → 44 treatment landing pages** already built, each interlinked to its category and related treatments. ([kirankumarvel](https://kirankumarvel.wordpress.com/2026/06/04/dental-seo-patient-first-content-authority/))

### 3.5 International / multilingual (the dental-tourism multiplier)
Without correct **hreflang**, a clinic's EN/IT/DE/FR pages **cannibalize each other** in Google. And **localization ≠ translation** — each language must target that market's real search intent. ([invigoMEDIA](https://invigomedia.com/medical-marketing/international-patient-seo/), [healthus](https://healthus.ai/seo-for-medical-tourism/))

### 3.6 Links *and* brand mentions
Backlinks still matter — but for AI visibility, **brand mentions correlate at 0.664 vs 0.218 for backlinks (3× stronger).** Unlinked mentions on trusted sites now pull real weight. ([upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/entity-authority-ai-citation-2026/), [techloy](https://www.techloy.com/how-to-build-brand-mentions-and-links-that-force-llms-chatgpt-gemini-perplexity-to-cite-and-recommend-your-brand/))

## Part 4 — The GEO layer (new, and measurable)

The **Princeton/Georgia Tech "GEO" study** (arXiv 2311.09735) tested 9 tactics across 10,000 queries. Proven, quantified lifts:
- Adding **statistics → +41%** citation visibility.
- Adding **quotations → +28%.**
- **Citing external sources → +115%** for lower-ranked pages.
- Overall up to **+40%** visibility — and **mid-ranked pages (~position 5) benefit most (+115%)**, while position-1 pages barely move. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735), [seo.ai](https://seo.ai/blog/generative-engine-optimization-geo))

**What this dictates:**
1. **Answer-ready content** — lead with a crisp definition/answer, then expand. FAQ blocks, clear headings, scannable facts. (Our treatment pages already do intro → benefits → process → FAQ.)
2. **Saturate pages with original stats and quotes** — patient outcomes, success rates, real testimonial quotes, cited authorities.
3. **Be present in each engine's preferred pool** — Wikipedia/Wikidata (ChatGPT), Reddit/forums (Perplexity), YouTube (AI Overviews), Trustpilot/Google reviews (all).
4. **Structured data is the GEO substrate** — it's how engines parse you into facts they can quote.
5. **Crawlability for AI bots** — allow GPTBot/PerplexityBot; consider an **`llms.txt`** map of key pages.

## Part 5 — The genius layer (asymmetric plays competitors miss)

These are the moves with outsized, compounding, hard-to-copy returns.

1. **Turn DMA's numbers into a citable data asset.** Original statistics are the #1 citation magnet — and DMA *owns* incredible ones: **38,000+ implants, 98% success, 8,000+ patients, since 2009.** Publish an annual, dated, source-able "**Dental Med Austria Outcomes Report**" page (and a stat block reused across the site). This is the single highest-leverage GEO move available to you — most clinics have nothing original to cite; you have a goldmine.

2. **The free remote plan as an engine, not a button.** A "free written plan in 24–48h" is both a conversion magnet *and* the kind of concrete, helpful answer AI loves to surface — and AI traffic converts at **14.2%.** Give it its own optimized page (already built: `/catalogue/remote-treatment-plan`) and weave it into every FAQ answer about cost.

3. **Own the queries AI actually answers: cost & "vs-country" comparisons.** People ask engines *"How much is All-on-4 in Albania vs the UK?"* Build honest, structured **cost** and **comparison** pages (Albania vs UK/Italy/Germany) with ranges, what's included, and the brands used. These are the exact prompts generative engines quote — and almost no competitor formats them well.

4. **Programmatic matrix: treatment × language × intent.** You already have the *treatment* axis (44 pages). Multiply it by **language** (EN/IT/DE/FR/AL with hreflang) and **intent** (cost / safety / recovery / vs-country). That's a defensible long-tail moat in markets where DMA's competitors are thin.

5. **Doctor-as-entity (E-E-A-T as a weapon).** Give **Dr. Mentor Zeqja** a real entity footprint — author bylines, an authoritative bio page, a **Wikidata item**, and a `sameAs` network. Firsthand, credentialed experience is exactly what 2025 E-E-A-T rewards and what YMYL demands.

6. **Entity seeding.** Close the "entity infrastructure gap": a clean **Wikidata** entry for the clinic + founder, consistent **NAP**, and a `sameAs` web (Google Business Profile, Trustpilot, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube). This is *why* AI picks one brand over an equally-good competitor.

7. **The review-velocity flywheel.** Reviews feed local rank (16%), credibility thresholds (50+ @4.3★), *and* the platforms each engine trusts. Systematically earn reviews on **Google, Trustpilot, Reddit threads, and YouTube testimonials** — one flywheel feeding every surface.

8. **Mine your "position 5" pages.** GEO lifts mid-ranked pages most (+115%). Find pages already ranking 4–10 and add stats/quotes/citations first — fastest wins.

## Part 6 — How to measure it

- **AI citation share / share-of-voice** — track how often DMA is named/cited across ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews (tools: Profound, Otterly, Peec, ZipTie, or manual prompt audits).
- **Rich-result coverage** — Google Search Console "Enhancements" + Rich Results Test.
- **Local actions** — GBP calls, direction requests, website clicks; review count & average.
- **Assisted conversions** — remote-plan submissions, contact form, by language/market.
- **Classic** — rankings, impressions/clicks (GSC), referral traffic from `chatgpt.com`, `perplexity.ai`, etc.
