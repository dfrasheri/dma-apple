## Implants (12)

### Premium Straumann BLX Implant
- desc: Featured Bookimed offer. Straumann BLX is the Swiss gold-standard implant — apically tapered, fully tapered design optimized for immediate placement and immediate loading. Lifetime warranty. The implant brand of choice when patients ask for the 'best' option.
- materials: Straumann Group (Swiss)

### Biodem Dental Implant
- desc: Featured Bookimed offer. Biodem is a German implant brand offered with lifetime warranty. Mid-premium tier between mass-market Asian implants and Straumann.
- materials: Biodem (Germany) — German manufacturing, CE marked, lifetime warranty, implant passport

### Single Implant + Abutment + Crown (full unit)
- desc: Complete fixed single-tooth solution: implant + healing cap + custom abutment + zirconia or e-max crown. The most common single-tooth tourism case.
- materials: Straumann/Biodem/ETK implant + Dental Direkt zirconia or Ivoclar E-max + Creation ceramic stain

### All-on-4 (single arch)
- desc: Four implants supporting fixed full-arch prosthesis. Same-day temporary teeth available via 3D-guided surgical technique. Flagship dental tourism procedure.
- materials: 4x Straumann/Biodem/ETK + multi-unit abutments + provisional + final zirconia/E-max bridge

### All-on-4 (both arches)
- desc: Both upper and lower arches restored with All-on-4. The most common medical-tourism All-on-X case.
- materials: 8x implants + 2 final full-arch bridges

### All-on-6 (single arch)
- desc: Six implants for stiffer, higher-force full-arch restoration.
- materials: 6x implants + multi-unit abutments + final bridge

### All-on-6 (both arches)
- desc: Six implants per arch on both jaws. Top-tier full-mouth tourism restoration.
- materials: 12x implants + 2 final bridges

### All-on-8 (single arch)
- desc: Eight implants for maximum-rigidity case (heavy bruxers, large arches).
- materials: 8x implants + 2 multi-unit bridges

### Mandibular Osteotomy / Orthognathic Surgery
- desc: Complex jaw repositioning surgery (BSSO/Le Fort I) performed by Dr. Alessio Osmani. Hospital-grade procedure. Treats mandibular protrusion/retrusion or open bite.
- materials: Osteosynthesis plates + sedation + anesthesiology team

### Bone Augmentation / GBR
- desc: Guided bone regeneration combining particulate bone graft with collagen membrane to grow back lost bone before implant placement.
- materials: Botiss Cerabone + Botiss Jason membrane

### Sinus Lift (open / lateral window)
- desc: Lateral-window approach to lift sinus floor for severely atrophic posterior maxilla.
- materials: Botiss Cerabone + membrane

### 3D Computer-Guided Implant Surgery
- desc: Pre-surgical CBCT planning + 3D-printed surgical guide + computer-controlled drill protocol. Increases precision dramatically.
- materials: Vatech CBCT + planning software + 3D printed guide

## Crowns & Aesthetics (12)
